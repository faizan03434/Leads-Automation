# Bytora Lead Finder

Run start.bat in this folder or python finder.py. Open http://127.0.0.1:8766.
The original Mail Studio on port 8765 stays separate.

Generate reads public OpenStreetMap listings through Overpass. Prefer a city and modest count; country queries can time out. Use exact local city names. Emails come from public contact tags. Missing website means not listed, not confirmed absent. Management systems are unknown. Results accumulate locally.

Optional Apollo: enter an eligible API key and per-search lookup cap. Exact company matches are used to enrich owner/founder/manager emails. Coverage, access and credit charges depend on your Apollo account. Free credit balance is not monitored; errors pause lookups. No paid subscription is purchased. Keys are Windows-DPAPI encrypted and excluded from Git.

Select email leads, add to campaign, review the separate template and recipients, confirm and send. Templates support {{name}}, {{niche}}, {{city}}, {{country}}, {{services}}. SMTP credentials are independently encrypted. Timeouts continue to the next lead; uncertain leads need a Sent-folder check before retry. Authentication failures stop. Search does not send emails.

No Google scraping, invented emails or WhatsApp integration. Public contacts do not establish marketing permission. Honor unsubscribe replies. Data and encrypted credentials stay local and are excluded from Git.

Business data: OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (ODbL).
